Privacy Policy

TerriStory ("the App") is operated in accordance with the following Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

The App collects the following information:

  • Nickname: A display name freely chosen by the player, used for multiplayer battles and the ranking feature
  • Gameplay data: Battle results, scores earned, ranking points, and similar data

Purpose of Use

Your nickname and gameplay data are sent to and stored on our server via Google Firebase (Realtime Database) for the following purposes:

  • Providing the online multiplayer battle feature
  • Providing the ranking (leaderboard) display feature
  • Improving the App

Your nickname may be displayed to your opponents and other players. We recommend that you do not use information that could identify you personally (such as your real name) as your nickname.

Disclosure to Third Parties

The App uses Google Firebase to manage data. This data handling is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Handling of Personal Information

The App does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.
